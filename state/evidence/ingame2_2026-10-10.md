## Correction of EV-004
EV-004 said the room's "wood turned grey". The Albedo view (image 27) shows the blocks are neutral white, so the
grey look was correct for the material. The real defects are brightness and contrast. Vanilla's warm look comes
from block light, which in vanilla turns warmer as it dims.

## Log findings
- No shader compile/link errors. Repeated NVIDIA warning C7050 "tbn might be used before being initialized"
  (sodium:chunk_shader_for_terrain/_cutout): harmless, fixed in 0.3.0.
- Sodium 0.8.12 on the test machine (compatibility.md pinned 0.8.7).

## Root causes (simulator calibrated first: the 0.2.1 model reproduces the morning shot within ~15%)
1. Reinhard tonemap compressed every mid-tone (scene value 1.0 shown at 0.5).
2. Exposure ignored the sun's height, so a low morning sun left the scene dark; the visible sky did not brighten with the sun either.
3. Light-level falloff was applied in linear light while Minecraft's curve is in display space, so mid light levels were ~5x brighter than vanilla (flat interiors).
4. Clouds were lit only by ambient + weak sun: grey by day, black at night.
