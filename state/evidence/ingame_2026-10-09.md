## Measured failure (image 2 vs vanilla image 1, sRGB, full frame)
| Metric | Vanilla | Catalyst 0.2.0-m2a |
|---|---|---|
| Mean luminance (linear) | 0.056 | 0.146 (2.6x) |
| Mean saturation | 0.39 | 0.15 |
| Left wall saturation | 0.33 | 0.02 (grey) |

Violates `docs/visual/aesthetic_direction.md` hard constraints "washed out" and "flat", plus "keep vanilla
materials almost completely recognizable" and exposure "very subtle by default".

## Root cause (reproduced by `tools/tone_sim.py --legacy`: room wall saturation 0.09)
1. Sky ambient used the raw, very saturated vanilla sky color (blue about 3.3x red) as its hue; blue light on warm albedo produced grey.
2. No interreflection: occluded interiors received only sky-colored light.
3. Sky ambient too weak (~10-14:1 sun-to-shade), so shade was near-black outdoors.
4. Full eye adaptation (about 4.4 stops) normalized dark interiors up to mid grey (washed out).

## Fix (0.2.1, `tools/tone_sim.py`, compile PASS 23 configurations)
Desaturated near-neutral sky ambient at a clear-day ~4.5:1 ratio; albedo-tinted multi-bounce occlusion
(Jimenez 2016) at 55%; neutral readability floor; partial eye adaptation (default 0.5) around a fixed daylight reference.
Simulated room floor: (0.33, 0.30, 0.22) vs vanilla (0.28, 0.22, 0.16); wall saturation 0.38 vs albedo 0.36.
**Needs an in-game re-test** (same room + an outdoor daytime shot with Debug View = Off).
