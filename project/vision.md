# Catalyst Product Vision

Catalyst is a Minecraft Java shader focused on preserving Minecraft identity while providing a coherent modern cinematic environmental renderer.

The visual target is approximately 60% natural/realistic environmental presentation and 40% cinematic enhancement, with cinematic intensity emerging from conditions rather than a permanent heavy filter. Gameplay readability is protected by default.

Catalyst should make lighting, atmosphere, weather, water, foliage, clouds, materials, shadows, and camera presentation feel like parts of one environmental system.
